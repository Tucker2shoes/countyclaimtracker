County Governments Are Holding Your Money

CountyClaimTracker.com — The Consolidated County Unclaimed Property Database

Most people know about state unclaimed property. Almost nobody knows that counties hold their own separate pool — stale checks, property tax refunds, surplus funds from tax sales. And unlike state funds, county funds expire.
